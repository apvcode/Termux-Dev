# Logo ideas for devx (replace the `>_` launcher icon)

Black rounded square, white mark, one grey accent. SVGs are in `logo-ideas/`; `logo-ideas-sheet.png` shows them side by side.

1. **dx monogram**: a geometric d and a smaller grey x. Plain, recognisable, works at any size.
2. **Bubble d**: a chat bubble with a d cut out of it. Says "chat" and "devx" at once.
3. **Prompt bubble**: a chevron and a block cursor inside a bubble outline. Terminal heritage without the underscore that looks like Termux.
4. **Link**: two overlapping rings, one white (the phone) and one grey (the computer). It is what the app is about, abstractly.
5. **Orbit**: a bright centre with a small satellite on an orbit. An agent working around your project.
6. **Serif d**: a single serif d, close to the serif the app uses for the assistant's words. Calm and editorial.
